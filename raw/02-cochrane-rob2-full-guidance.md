# Source 02: Revised Cochrane Risk-of-Bias Tool for Randomized Trials (RoB 2) — Full Guidance Document

- **Citation**: Higgins JPT, Savović J, Page MJ, Sterne JAC, on behalf of the RoB 2 Development Group. *Revised Cochrane risk-of-bias tool for randomized trials (RoB 2): Full guidance document*. Version of 22 August 2019.
- **Official URL**: https://www.riskofbias.info/welcome/rob-2-0-tool/current-version-of-rob-2
- **Key Operational Guidance**:
  - **Effect of interest (D2)**: Assessors must specify upfront whether they are evaluating the *effect of assignment to intervention* (intention-to-treat, ITT) or the *effect of adhering to intervention* (per-protocol).
  - **Allocation concealment (D1)**: Adequate random sequence generation alone is insufficient; concealing the upcoming allocation sequence until enrollment is critical to prevent selection bias. Baseline imbalances that are incompatible with chance suggest failure of randomization/concealment.
  - **Missing outcome data (D3)**: Availability of data for nearly all randomized participants (commonly >95% for continuous outcomes or low proportion relative to event rate for dichotomous outcomes) supports low risk; otherwise, assessors must check whether missingness depends on the true outcome value.
  - **Outcome measurement (D4)**: Distinguishes whether the outcome assessor was aware of the intervention received AND whether assessment of the outcome could have been influenced by knowledge of intervention received.
  - **Selective reporting (D5)**: Requires comparing reported numerical results and analyses against a pre-specified statistical analysis plan (SAP) or trial registry finalized before unblinded outcome data were available.
