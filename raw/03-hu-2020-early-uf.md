# Source 03: Efficacy and Safety of Early Ultrafiltration in Patients with Acute Decompensated Heart Failure with Volume Overload

- **Original URL**: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12872-020-01733-5
- **Citation**: Hu J, Wan Q, Zhang Y, Zhou J, Li M, Jiang L, Yuan F. Efficacy and safety of early ultrafiltration in patients with acute decompensated heart failure with volume overload: a prospective, randomized, controlled clinical trial. *BMC Cardiovascular Disorders*. 2020;20:447. DOI: 10.1186/s12872-020-01733-5.
- **Trial Registration**: Chinese Clinical Trial Registry `ChiCTR2000030696`.
- **Study Design**: Prospective, randomized, controlled, open-label clinical trial.
- **Population**: Hospitalized patients with acute decompensated heart failure (ADHF) and volume overload within 24 hours of admission.
- **Sample Size**: Total **100 patients** with uneven group allocation:
  - Early Ultrafiltration (UF) group: `n = 40`
  - Control group (Torasemide plus Tolvaptan): `n = 60`
- **Blinding**: Open-label / unblinded (extracorporeal mechanical ultrafiltration vs. oral/IV pharmacologic regimen).
- **Outcomes**: Short-term decongestion metrics (body weight loss, urine output, dyspnea/congestion score, serum creatinine at day 4 and day 8) plus 1-month and 3-month readmission and mortality follow-up.
- **Methodological Notes for RoB 2**:
  - Unequal group allocation (`40` vs `60`) with limited detail on random sequence generation and allocation concealment in primary report.
  - Open-label intervention; objective endpoints (weight, creatinine, urine output) coexist with more subjective symptom assessments.
  - Trial registration exists (`ChiCTR2000030696`), allowing cross-check of pre-specified outcomes (also check timing of registration).
