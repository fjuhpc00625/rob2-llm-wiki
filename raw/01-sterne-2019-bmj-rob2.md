# Source 01: RoB 2: A Revised Tool for Assessing Risk of Bias in Randomised Trials (BMJ 2019)

- **Citation**: Sterne JAC, Savović J, Page MJ, Elbers RG, Blencowe NS, Boutron I, Cates CJ, Cheng HY, Corbett MS, Eldridge SM, Emberson JR, Hernán MA, Hopewell S, Hróbjartsson A, Junqueira DR, Jüni P, Kirkham JJ, Lasserson T, Li T, McAleenan A, Reeves BC, Shepperd S, Shrier I, Stewart LA, Tilling K, White IR, Whiting PF, Higgins JPT. RoB 2: a revised tool for assessing risk of bias in randomised trials. *BMJ*. 2019;366:l4898.
- **DOI**: https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.l4898
- **Core Contribution**:
  - Introduces the revised Cochrane Risk of Bias tool (RoB 2) structured into **five fixed domains** of bias for individually randomized, parallel-group trials:
    1. Bias arising from the randomization process (D1)
    2. Bias due to deviations from intended interventions (D2)
    3. Bias due to missing outcome data (D3)
    4. Bias in measurement of the outcome (D4)
    5. Bias in selection of the reported result (D5)
  - Shifts assessment to the **result / outcome level** (rather than whole-study level), because bias can differ across outcomes within the same trial (e.g., objective mortality vs. subjective symptom score).
  - Introduces explicit **signaling questions** (`Yes`, `Probably yes`, `Probably no`, `No`, `No information`) mapped via deterministic **algorithms** to domain-level judgements (`Low risk of bias`, `Some concerns`, `High risk of bias`).
