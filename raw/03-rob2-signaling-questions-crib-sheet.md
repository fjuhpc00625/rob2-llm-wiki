# Source 03: RoB 2 Crib Sheet & Signaling Questions Reference (22 Signaling Questions)

- **Citation**: RoB 2 Development Group. *RoB 2 Crib Sheet for Individually Randomized, Parallel-Group Trials*. Cochrane Methods Bias Group (2019).
- **Official URL**: https://www.riskofbias.info/welcome/rob-2-0-tool/current-version-of-rob-2
- **Response Options**: `Y` (Yes), `PY` (Probably yes), `PN` (Probably no), `N` (No), `NI` (No information), `NA` (Not applicable when conditional gate is skipped).
- **Key Signaling Questions by Domain**:
  - **D1 (Randomization)**: `1.1` Was the allocation sequence random? `1.2` Was the allocation sequence concealed until participants were enrolled and assigned? `1.3` Did baseline differences between intervention groups suggest a problem with the randomization process?
  - **D2 (Deviations — Effect of Assignment / ITT)**: `2.1` Were participants aware of their assigned intervention? `2.2` Were carers and people delivering the interventions aware? `2.3` (If Y/PY/NI to 2.1 or 2.2) Were there deviations from the intended intervention that arose because of the trial context? `2.4` Were these deviations likely to have affected the outcome? `2.5` Were these deviations balanced between groups? `2.6` Was an appropriate analysis used to estimate the effect of assignment (e.g., ITT)? `2.7` Was there potential for a substantial impact of the failure to analyse participants in the group to which they were randomized?
  - **D3 (Missing Outcome Data)**: `3.1` Were data for this outcome available for all, or nearly all, participants randomized? `3.2` Is there evidence that the result was not biased by missing outcome data? `3.3` Could missingness in the outcome depend on its true value? `3.4` Is it likely that missingness in the outcome depended on its true value?
  - **D4 (Outcome Measurement)**: `4.1` Was the method of measuring the outcome inappropriate? `4.2` Could measurement or ascertainment of the outcome have differed between intervention groups? `4.3` Were outcome assessors aware of the intervention received? `4.4` (If Y/PY/NI to 4.3) Could assessment of the outcome have been influenced by knowledge of intervention received? `4.5` (If Y/PY/NI to 4.4) Is it likely that assessment of the outcome was influenced by knowledge of intervention received?
  - **D5 (Selection of Reported Result)**: `5.1` Were the data that produced this result analysed in accordance with a pre-specified analysis plan finalized before unblinded outcome data were available? `5.2` Is the numerical result likely to have been selected from multiple eligible outcome measurements? `5.3` Is the numerical result likely to have been selected from multiple eligible analyses of the data?
