# Source 05: Once-Daily Single-Inhaler Triple Therapy (FF/UMEC/VI) vs. FF/VI in Inadequately Controlled Asthma (The CAPTAIN Trial)

- **Original URL**: https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lanres/article/PIIS2213-2600(20)30389-1/abstract
- **Citation**: Lee LA, Bailes Z, Barnes N, Boulet LP, Edwards D, Fowler A, Hanania NA, Kerstjens HAM, Kerwin E, Nathan R, Oppenheimer J, Papi A, Pascoe S, Brusselle G, Peachey G, Sule N, Tabberer M, Pavord ID, et al. Efficacy and safety of once-daily single-inhaler triple therapy (FF/UMEC/VI) versus FF/VI in patients with inadequately controlled asthma (CAPTAIN): a double-blind, randomised, phase 3A trial. *The Lancet Respiratory Medicine*. 2021;9(1):69–84. (Epub 2020 Sep 9). DOI: 10.1016/S2213-2600(20)30389-1. PMID: 32918892.
- **Study Design**: International, multicenter (416 study centers across 15 countries), randomized (1:1:1:1:1:1), **double-blind**, active-controlled, parallel-group, Phase 3A clinical trial.
- **Population**: Adult patients with inadequately controlled asthma despite ICS/LABA therapy.
- **Sample Size**: Total **2,436 patients** randomized across 6 once-daily treatment arms delivered via identical single Ellipta dry-powder inhalers.
- **Intervention vs. Control**: FF/UMEC/VI (4 dose combinations) vs. FF/VI (2 dose combinations), all once daily via matching Ellipta inhaler.
- **Blinding**: **Double-blind** (patients, investigators, and outcome assessors masked via identical-appearing single-inhaler delivery and centralized interactive randomization).
- **Primary Endpoint**: Change from baseline in clinic trough forced expiratory volume in 1 second (FEV1) at Week 24.
- **Methodological Notes for RoB 2**:
  - Serves as a **Low Risk of Bias benchmark** in this Wiki: centralized randomization & allocation concealment (D1 Low), double-blind matching inhaler & ITT analysis (D2 Low), rigorous Phase 3A trial follow-up & handling of missing data (D3 Low), objective spirometry FEV1 with blinded assessors (D4 Low), and pre-registered Phase 3A protocol & statistical analysis plan (D5 Low).
